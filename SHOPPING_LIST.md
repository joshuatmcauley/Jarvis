## 💡 Money-Saving Tips

1. **Buy Pi from official retailers** (Pimoroni, Pi Hut) - avoid scalpers
2. **Check Amazon Warehouse** for open-box displays (20-30% off)
3. **AliExpress for cameras** if you can wait 6-8 weeks (half price)
4. **eBay for OBD-II adapters** - generic ELM327 work fine for basic use
5. **Salvage yard for wire** - grab wire from scrapped cars (free!)
6. **Borrow tools** from friends or local maker space
7. **3D print enclosure** at local library/maker space (usually free/cheap)
8. **Buy in phases** - get core system running first, add cameras later

---

## ⚠️ Things NOT to Cheap Out On

1. **Power supply** - Bad buck converters can fry your Pi (stick to known brands)
2. **SD card/SSD** - Cheap storage = data corruption (Samsung/SanDisk only)
3. **Fuses** - Always use proper automotive fuses
4. **Screen** - Low-nit displays are unreadable in sun (600+ nits minimum)
5. **Crimping tool** - Bad crimps = unreliable connections
