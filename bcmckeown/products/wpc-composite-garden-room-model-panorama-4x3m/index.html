Panorama 13.44 m² WPC Garden Room – Premium Quality, Modern Design

The Panorama 13.44 m² WPC Garden Room is a premium, high-performance outdoor building that meets the highest standards in design, durability, and functionality. Made from co-extruded 28 mm WPC hollow-chamber profiles, it offers exceptional stability while providing generous space for storage, hobbies, or outdoor living.

Its modern corner joints with aluminum trim give the structure a clean, contemporary look. The wide aluminum double door, equipped with a high-quality handle, cylinder lock, and safety-glass glazing, ensures both security and style. Two floor-to-ceiling fixed windows let in abundant natural light, creating a bright and inviting interior.

The shed also includes a side-opening aluminum skylight window, a UV- and weather-resistant plastic roof, and a complete metal gutter system with downpipe. The elegant anthracite/gray color combination and high-quality WPC-aluminum construction give the shed a sophisticated, modern appearance that complements any garden.

Features

This premium garden room combines engineered durability with sophisticated design. The 4x3M structure features 28mm co-extruded WPC hollow-chamber walls that resist moisture, UV damage, and color fading whilst maintaining a natural wood aesthetic. Aluminum framing throughout—including corner joints, roof rafters, and base—ensures structural integrity and level installation. The glazing specification includes a high-quality aluminum double door with cylinder lock, one opening skylight window, and two fixed floor-to-ceiling windows, all fitted with clear safety glass for optimal light and visibility. An integrated aluminum gutter system with downpipe manages water drainage effectively. The weather-resistant plastic roof and ventilation grilles maintain interior airflow and protection. WPC construction eliminates traditional maintenance requirements—no painting, varnishing, or oiling needed—whilst providing exceptional resistance to warping, cracking, splintering, mold, and pest damage. Assembly hardware and detailed instructions are supplied; flooring is not included.
