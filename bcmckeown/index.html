About Us

B&C McKeown Ltd is a trusted and long-established supplier located in Downpatrick, County Down, Northern Ireland. With over 30 years of industry experience, we proudly serve a diverse clientele, including homeowners, contractors, developers, and commercial enterprises across the region. Our reputation is built on reliability, quality, and exceptional customer service. We specialize in providing high-quality outdoor building materials that are designed to enhance both the functionality and aesthetic of any property. From durable roofing solutions and precision-cut roof cladding to elegant granite paving, innovative composite decking, fencing systems, and secure mesh fencing options—we offer a comprehensive range of products to meet all your exterior building needs. Whether you're undertaking a residential project or a large-scale commercial development, B&C McKeown Ltd is your go-to partner for dependable materials and expert guidance.
