19.2mm Composite Wood Garden Shed – 4.51m²

B&C Mckeown Skylight Pent Roof

This latest addition to the B&C Mckeown range of composite wood garden sheds perfectly combines modern design with outstanding durability. Designed to complement contemporary outdoor spaces, these sheds feature clean vertical lines, sharp angles, a sleek flat pent roof, and on-trend garden colours.

Premium Construction & Performance

Built using advanced co-extruded composite technology, the shed offers exceptional strength and longevity. The high-quality resin-wood blend includes an additional protective layer, providing superior resistance to moisture, stains, and everyday wear.

Key functional advantages include:

  • Unmatched durability thanks to a state-of-the-art composite made from premium materials

  • Fully waterproof roof, eliminating the need for extra roof coverings and reducing overall costs

  • High-quality double doors for easy access to bulky or heavy items, fitted with a secure key lock

  • Solid door design on the Gordon version to ensure maximum privacy for stored contents

Versatile & Practical

The Gordon composite shed from Woodlife Garden is a standout addition to any garden. Ideal for storing DIY tools, gardening equipment, and outdoor furniture during winter, it can also be used as a compact workshop or to protect swimming pool technical equipment.

Technical Specifications

  • Overall external dimensions (W x D): 237 x 223 cm

  • External dimensions (W x D): 212.4 x 212.4 cm

  • Internal dimensions (W x D): 206 x 206 cm

  • Wall height: 200.8 cm

  • Ridge height: 226.6 cm

  • Double door size: 139.8 x 187.5 cm

  • Gutter and downpipe: Included

  • Doors: Aluminium with lock (installable on front or sides, not rear)

  • Glazing: 2.5 mm acrylic

  • Roof: Painted metal, fully waterproof

  • Roof pitch: Can slope forward or backward

  • Snow load capacity: 50 kg/m²

  • Material composition: 60% wood, 30% polymer, 10% additives

  • Roof colour: Anthracite exterior, white interior

A true centrepiece for modern gardens, this composite shed delivers style, security, and long-lasting performance.
