## Quick answers to keep honest

| They ask | You say |
|----------|---------|
| Are you a network engineer? | I am a CS grad completing a network engineering traineeship. I want a year on the desk / as a technician so I am useful on real faults before I own the LAN. |
| Why only Tech+ / A+? | Year-one stack is A+, Network+, Security+, AZ-900, CCNA. I am not sitting three exams at once. |
| Do you have a car? | Driving test booked **4 November 2026** (update after the day). Train to Belfast from Ballymoney is usable for station-side roles. |
| Why should we hire a chef? | Shift discipline, procedures under pressure, customer-facing, documenting when service goes wrong. Then point at the lab. |
